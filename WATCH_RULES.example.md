Ping me when ANY of these happen:

- A new inbound message from a customer arrives in a conversation.
- A conversation gets assigned to me.

Do NOT ping for:

- Internal notes or replies written by teammates.
- Status, tag, or priority changes.
- "Someone is typing" indicators.
- Counters, timestamps, or other UI noise changing.

When unsure, answer NO.
